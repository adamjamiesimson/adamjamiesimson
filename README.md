## 👋 About Me

I’m an engineering student and independent researcher based in Dubai. I enjoy turning ambitious ideas into practical, polished technology at the intersection of **AI, accessibility, science, and learning**.

## 🚀 Current Focus

- Building **[SignRelay](https://github.com/adamjamiesimson/SignRelay)**, a real-time multilingual sign-language translation platform.
- Co-founding **[BookQuest](https://bookquestgame.vercel.app)**, an interactive game-based learning platform.
- Strengthening my engineering foundations in mathematics, physics, and problem-solving.
- Exploring applied research that can contribute to better, more accessible technology.
